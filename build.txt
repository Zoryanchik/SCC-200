1. Start pgsql server, run:
    ./transport-backend/run_pgsql.sh
Wait until you see "Postgres is ready and listening on localhost:5011"

2. Start OSRM server, run:
    ./transport-backend/osrm/run_osrm.sh
Wait until you see "[info] Listening on: 0.0.0.0:5012
                    [info] running and waiting for requests"

OPEN A NEW TERMINAL

3. Start backend server, run:
    ./transport-backend/run_backend.sh
Wait until you see "Backend is ready at http://localhost:5050"

4. Start frontend server, run:
    ./transport-frontend/run_frontend.sh
Wait until you see "--------------- Frontend is ready. ---------------
                    --------------- URL: http://localhost:5075 ---------------"

Click or go to http://localhost:5075 to visit the app
