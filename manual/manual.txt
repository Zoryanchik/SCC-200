In home page:


For rouing:

To do routing from current location starting now, simply input destination into the "To" bar,
choose the right one from the prompts, and click "Search routes".

To view results, scroll down and see results in "Suggested routes" card.

Scroll in "Suggest routes" card to compare results, click on one result to view its track.

Click on "Fold" to temporarily hide the routes card, or click on "Close" to close it.

For different routing, click on "use current location" to auto fill "From" location.

Click on the switch button between "From" location bar and "To" location bar to switch locations in them.

Adjust start time, mode limit and transfer limit using the drag down boxes below.

Click the "Now" button to quickly set start time to now.


For map and live vehicles:

Hover over buses to see info, click on the bus to show its route track.

Hover over or click on a train station to see its departures.

Use "Buses", "Trains" filters to filter out buses or train.

Click "Off-line" button to show or hide off-line buses.

Click "Centre on me" to zoom the map to current location

Click weather button to show weather details

Scroll down to bottom of page to see service alerts at tarin stations and upcoming departure at train stations.

Set different font size, to light/dark mode, or to contrast mode by clicking the buttons at right top of page

Click "Map" at right top of page to visit the map page.

Click "use current location" to zoom to current location, and display nearby stops

Input a location in the search bar to zoom to and inspect the location on map
